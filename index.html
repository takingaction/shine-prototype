How it works

We bring the creative. You bring the room.

We bring the artistic vision, creative tools, and a proven six-day process. You bring the space, the participants, and your community. Together, we turn a blank page into opening night.

We bring

  • Brian Feinstein as lead teaching artist and music director, on-site throughout the intensive
  • The SHINE curriculum, creative exercises, and songwriting and storytelling tools
  • A flexible production roadmap, from first ideas to final performance
  • Marketing support, including promotional copy and customizable show artwork
  • Artistic guidance throughout the week, culminating in an original musical created by your participants

You bring

  • A staff lead and 1–2 teaching assistants to support the creative process
  • A rehearsal space suitable for your group size
  • A stage or performance space for the final presentation
  • A piano or full-size keyboard
  • Registration and ticketing through your organization’s channels

The six-day creative journey

  1. Scene 1 · Day 1

    The Blank Page

    The ensemble forms, games begin, and the first ideas fly.

  2. Scene 2 · Day 2

    Characters & Story

    Together, they choose their story and invent the characters who’ll bring it to life.

  3. Scene 3 · Day 3

    Writing the Songs

    Lyrics and melodies take shape as their ideas come alive at the piano.

  4. Scene 4 · Day 4

    Bringing Scenes to Life

    Dialogue, movement, and music meet as the story finds its feet.

  5. Scene 5 · Day 5

    Putting It All Together

    Run-throughs, costumes, props, and opening-night butterflies.

  6. Opening Night Scene 6 · Day 6

    Opening Night!

    From a blank page to a world premiere. Their story. Their songs. Your stage.

Typical intensive: Six days, with a final performance on Day 6. Daily hours and performance times are coordinated with the host venue.

Where It All Began: The Broward Center

In summer 2026, the Broward Center for the Performing Arts hosted the inaugural SHINE intensive, bringing together seventeen young theatre-makers to create something entirely their own.

Six days later, they took the stage with The Mystery of Mockingbird Manor, an original musical filled with their characters, their story, and their songs, performed for an audience of family and friends.

What began as a blank page became a world premiere. And an unforgettable experience for everyone involved.

The SHINE Experience

Everyone has a story worth telling.

On day one, there’s no script. No songs. Just a room full of young imaginations and a blank page.

Six days later? A world premiere.

Together, our young theatre-makers dream up characters, build a story, write original songs, and bring their very own musical to life onstage, in front of family and friends.

No prior experience necessary. Just curiosity, creativity, and a willingness to jump in.

Brian will be there every step of the way, guiding the creative process, exploring their ideas at the piano, and helping them discover something pretty extraordinary: the power of their own imagination and what they can create together.

And yes, there will be plenty of laughter along the way.

The details

  • Ages: Vary by program and location
  • No writing, musical or performance experience necessary
  • Six days. One original musical. Their world premiere.

What they’ll take home (besides the applause)

  • Confidence: The thrill of sharing something they’ve created on a professional stage.
  • Creative skills: Hands-on experience with storytelling, songwriting, character development, and collaboration.
  • A sense of ownership: Their characters. Their lyrics. Their musical.
  • An ensemble: New friendships and the discovery of what happens when creative minds come together.
  • A lasting memory: A world premiere that began with their own ideas.

Meet The Creator

Brian Feinstein.

“Creativity isn’t taught. It’s unleashed.”

Brian Feinstein is an award-winning composer, producer, and educator whose work spans Broadway-bound musicals, Off-Broadway productions, and stages across the country. His credits include the upcoming musical adaptation of The Bad News Bears at A.C.T. in San Francisco, Mimi Le Duck, starring the legendary Eartha Kitt, and music performed by the Boston Pops. A Jonathan Larson Fellow and founder of FeinLine Academy, Brian is also the author of The FeinLine Philosophy.

After more than 25 years of working with young artists, he created SHINE around one simple idea: give people the tools, the freedom, and the confidence to trust their own creative voices. Then watch what happens when their imaginations take center stage.

FAQ

Questions, Answered.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend reaching out several months before your preferred dates to allow time for planning, registration, and coordinating with your team. Have something sooner in mind? Let’s talk.

What is the financial model?

SHINE is customized for each host organization, with pricing based on program size, location, and production needs. Host organizations may offset program costs through registration, ticket sales, and local sponsorships. We’ll explore the options together.

What kind of space do we need?

A rehearsal space large enough for the ensemble to move, create, and rehearse comfortably, plus a stage or suitable performance space for the final presentation.

Does Brian travel?

Yes. Brian personally leads SHINE intensives on-site at the host venue. Travel arrangements and expenses are addressed in the customized proposal.

Do we need a piano?

Yes. An acoustic piano or full-size keyboard is an essential part of the SHINE creative process, allowing students to explore their musical ideas throughout the week.

How is the program marketed?

FeinLine provides promotional guidance, copy, and available creative assets to support your outreach. Your organization manages local promotion, registration, and ticketing.

Does my child need singing, acting, or songwriting experience?

Not at all! SHINE welcomes first-timers and experienced performers alike. Curiosity, imagination, and a willingness to jump in are all they need. We’ll explore storytelling, songwriting, and musical theatre together, with a professional composer at the piano.

What ages is SHINE for?

SHINE is designed for young artists of all ages. Any age, any dream! Each host sets the exact age range for its session, so check with your local host for details.

What does a typical day look like?

Mornings are for brainstorming, theater exercises, storytelling, and songwriting. Afternoons get everyone on their feet, creating characters and staging scenes. By the end of the week, it all comes together for opening night. Daily hours are set by the host venue.

How much does it cost?

Tuition is set by each host organization. Details are shared when registration opens.

How do I sign my child up?

SHINE is presented in partnership with theatres, schools, and arts organizations, with registration handled directly by each host. Leave your email in our family interest form, and we’ll let you know when a SHINE intensive is planned near you.
