How it works

We bring the creative. You bring the room.

A clear split of responsibilities, then six days from a blank page to opening night.

We bring

  • Brian Feinstein as lead artist and music director, on-site all week
  • The full SHINE curriculum, sheet music, and lyric frameworks
  • A production playbook and staging plan
  • A host marketing toolkit (photos, video, copy)
  • Show-branded key art and posters
  • Liability insurance and COI documentation
  • A recording of the performance and a post-show debrief

You bring

  • A rehearsal room large enough for 20 students
  • A stage for the Day 6 performance
  • A piano or full-size keyboard
  • 1-2 on-site teaching assistants
  • Registration and ticketing through your channels
  • Your audience: families, donors, and press

FAQ

Questions hosts ask most.

How far in advance do we need to book?

Most hosts book 3-6 months in advance. Summer intensives typically confirm by February; school-year intensives by the start of the prior term.

What is the financial model?

Partnership pricing varies by cohort size and travel. Most hosts fund SHINE through student registration, opening-night ticket sales, and local sponsorship. We'll walk through the numbers on a short call.

What kind of space do we need?

A rehearsal room large enough for 20 students to move, and a theater or auditorium for the final performance with lighting and seating for family and friends.

Does Brian travel?

Yes. Brian leads every intensive on-site at the host venue. Travel is included in the partnership quote.

Do we need a piano?

An acoustic piano or full-size keyboard is required. Brian composes and rehearses at the instrument throughout the week.

How is the program marketed?

FeinLine provides a host toolkit with photography, video clips, and ready-to-use copy. Your team handles registration, ticketing, and local outreach.

What about insurance and liability?

FeinLine carries professional liability insurance and can provide a COI naming your organization as additional insured for the intensive week.

Will the performance be recorded?

Yes. Hosts and families receive a recording of the final performance along with the original script the ensemble created.

Who owns the musical the students create?

The original script and songs remain part of the ensemble's portfolio. Each student receives a printed copy and a recording as a keepsake.

Family-facing materials included

We answer your parents' questions for you. Hosts receive a ready-to-send family guide covering experience requirements, what to bring, the daily schedule, and the performance.
